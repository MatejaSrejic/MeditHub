Study Materials

Explore educational resources, publications, and professional materials related to digital health, MedTech, and innovation.

Shared knowledge, more ways to learn

Face-to-face exchange, online collaboration and independent study – MedIT-HuB brings learning to life through three connected stages. Each draws on the same coursebook, intended learning outcomes and core teaching materials. The delivery format and learning activities are adapted to make the most of each setting.

01

Masterclass

Live dialogue, shared ideas

Live lectures and seminars give participants the opportunity to ask questions, share their experiences and explore topics together. Sessions take place in person in Romania and Hungary, and through live virtual classrooms in Serbia.

02

BIP

Prepare online, explore together

The Blended Intensive Programme (BIP) combines self-paced online theory with in-person seminars and collaborative virtual activities. Participants build on their individual preparation through peer learning and joint work.

03

E-learning

Learn at your own pace

The fully online stage offers flexible access to digital course materials. Self-assessment activities, individual assignments and final submissions help participants deepen their understanding and demonstrate their learning.

Three learning formats, one shared academic standard

MedIT-HuB aims to ensure equivalent learning outcomes, accessible materials, a manageable workload and consistent assessment standards across all three stages.
