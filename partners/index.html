Core Partner Organisations

TAM CERT

TAM CERT Magyarország Vizsgáló és Tanúsító Kft. · Budapest, Hungary

Organisation Profile

TAM CERT is Europe's first accredited GDPR certification body, specialising in medical IT compliance, cybersecurity, and AI governance. The organisation conducts professional training programmes for companies, universities, and healthcare institutions in medical IT compliance, AI governance, ethical AI deployment, and regulatory frameworks for digital transformation.

With approximately 20–30 professionals including cybersecurity specialists, regulatory compliance experts, and digital health consultants, TAM CERT trains 100+ industry professionals and students annually in medical IT security and compliance.

GDPR Certification ISO 42001 (AI) IEC 62443 ISO 13485 MDR Compliance ISO 27001

Role in MedIT-HuB

  • WP1Leads project management, quality assurance, and regulatory alignment
  • WP2Develops modules on MedTech compliance, cybersecurity, and AI regulations
  • WP3Supports BIP piloting and quality control
  • WP4Leads MedTech Innovation Hub establishment and dissemination

EDU-PORT

Non-governmental Organisation · Subotica, Serbia

Organisation Profile

EDU-PORT is a non-governmental, non-profit organisation founded in 2015 with a mission to improve the quality of education at all levels — from primary to higher education and adult learning. The organisation focuses on teacher training, educational cooperation, innovative pedagogical methods, and fostering multicultural learning environments.

With a team of 15 educational professionals and an active network of educators, students, and community members, EDU-PORT annually reaches hundreds of learners through training sessions, courses, and professional events. The organisation brings strong experience in international higher-education cooperation projects.

Teacher Training BIP Design Digital Learning International Cooperation Multicultural Education

Role in MedIT-HuB

  • WP2Leads BIP development, integrating bioinformatics, applied informatics, and automation into medical IT education
  • WP2Organises study visits to China in coordination with Beijing JISIHULIAN
  • WP3Provides instructor training and student engagement strategies
  • WP4Supports dissemination and communication activities

Sapientia University

Universitatea Sapientia din Municipiul Cluj-Napoca · Cluj-Napoca, Romania

Organisation Profile

Sapientia University is a Hungarian-language university in Cluj-Napoca, Romania, with strong expertise in applied informatics, bioinformatics, genetic engineering, and biotechnology. The university provides research-driven course content and ensures alignment with academic standards in the MedTech domain.

With extensive experience in cross-border higher education projects, Sapientia brings proven expertise in curriculum development, academic quality assurance, and programme implementation.

Bioinformatics Applied Informatics Genetic Engineering Biotechnology ECTS Compliance

Role in MedIT-HuB

  • WP2 Defines modular curriculum framework, ensures ECTS alignment for accreditation
  • WP2 Leads digital content creation, integrating applied informatics and bioinformatics
  • WP3 Leads the BIP pilot phase — instructor training, student engagement, and evaluation
  • WP3 Implements and evaluates the pilot programme, ensuring scalability for EU-wide adoption

Expertise Areas

Automation, applied informatics, genetic engineering, biotechnology, and medical IT research — directly relevant to the MedTech BIP curriculum.
