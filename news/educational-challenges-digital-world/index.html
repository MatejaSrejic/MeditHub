Educational Challenges of the Digital World

Developing Digital and Blended Learning for MedTech Education

1 September 2026

Educational Challenges of the Digital World

MedTech Education, Train-the-Trainer and Professional Networking Conference

Date: 7 September 2026

Venue: Wekerle International University

Working languages: Hungarian and English

Application deadline: 1 September 2026

About the Event

TAM CERT, in cooperation with Wekerle International University and the participating consortium partners, invites higher-education professionals, researchers, trainers, lecturers, instructional designers and educational experts to contribute to the conference “Educational Challenges of the Digital World.”

The event is organised in connection with an Erasmus+ KA2 Cooperation Partnership in Higher Education. The project focuses on the development of e-learning and blended-learning materials in the field of medical technology, or MedTech, and the conference forms part of the consortium’s Train-the-Trainer, professional capacity-building and expert-engagement activities.

The project brings together academic and non-academic partners with complementary expertise. As consortium lead, TAM CERT coordinates the initiative and seeks to engage education specialists who can contribute pedagogical knowledge, methodological experience and practical insight to the development and delivery of high-quality MedTech learning materials.

The conference format will create a platform for sharing research, teaching practices and professional experience while building a network of experts who may contribute to the project’s educational, curriculum-development and Train-the-Trainer activities.

Purpose of the Conference

Medical technology is a rapidly developing and interdisciplinary field. Its effective teaching requires the integration of technical, clinical, regulatory and practical knowledge, supported by appropriate digital learning methods.

The conference will explore how e-learning and blended-learning approaches can support MedTech education and how educators, trainers and institutions can respond to the broader challenges created by digital transformation.

The event has five main objectives:

  1. To establish a network of experts interested in future educational and project cooperation.
  2. To present research findings and practical approaches related to digital and blended education.
  3. To gather pedagogical and methodological input for the development of MedTech learning materials.
  4. To identify good practices that can support the project’s Train-the-Trainer activities.
  5. To facilitate knowledge exchange between academic, training, healthcare, technology and professional communities.

Call for Contributions

We invite proposals for presentations addressing the educational challenges and opportunities associated with digital transformation, with particular attention to MedTech education, e-learning, blended learning and professional training.

Contributions may present:

  • academic or applied research;
  • e-learning and blended-learning methodologies;
  • approaches to teaching complex or interdisciplinary MedTech content;
  • curriculum-development methods;
  • instructional-design practices;
  • Train-the-Trainer models;
  • institutional experience;
  • case studies;
  • innovative educational tools and platforms;
  • assessment and quality-assurance approaches;
  • examples of good practice;
  • proposals for future professional or institutional cooperation.

Suggested Topics

Relevant topics include, but are not limited to:

  • e-learning and blended-learning models in MedTech education;
  • digital pedagogy and innovative teaching methods;
  • instructional design for technical, clinical and interdisciplinary subjects;
  • the development of digital MedTech learning materials;
  • Train-the-Trainer approaches in higher education and professional training;
  • teaching medical technologies to learners from different professional backgrounds;
  • curriculum design for rapidly changing technological fields;
  • simulation, virtual laboratories and practice-based digital learning;
  • artificial intelligence in teaching, learning and assessment;
  • digital competencies of educators, trainers and learners;
  • online, hybrid and blended-learning delivery models;
  • learner engagement and motivation in digital environments;
  • assessment of practical and technical competencies;
  • quality assurance in e-learning and blended learning;
  • accessibility and inclusive digital education;
  • micro-credentials and flexible learning pathways;
  • data protection, cybersecurity and ethical challenges;
  • regulatory and compliance-related education in MedTech;
  • collaboration between higher-education institutions, healthcare organisations, certification bodies and industry;
  • transferring academic knowledge into professional and industry-based training;
  • international and interdisciplinary approaches to MedTech education.

Proposals addressing other areas relevant to the overall theme are also welcome.

Expected Outcomes

The conference is expected to contribute to:

  • the pedagogical and methodological development of the project’s MedTech learning materials;
  • the exchange of knowledge and good practices in e-learning and blended learning;
  • the professional development of trainers and educators;
  • the identification of experts who may support content development, review or delivery;
  • the development of methods suitable for Train-the-Trainer activities;
  • stronger cooperation between academic and non-academic organisations;
  • closer links between education, healthcare, certification and the MedTech sector;
  • the creation of a professional network around the consortium;
  • possible future joint training, research and project activities.

Join the Professional Community

We warmly invite educators, researchers, instructional designers and MedTech professionals who would like to share their knowledge, contribute to the development of digital learning materials and participate in Train-the-Trainer activities.

The event is not only an opportunity to present research or good practices. It is also an invitation to become involved in a developing international community working to create accessible, relevant and high-quality e-learning and blended-learning solutions for MedTech education.
