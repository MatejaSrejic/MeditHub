MedIT-HuB: Advancing Digital Health Education Through International Collaboration

Connecting education, industry expertise, and international cooperation in digital health and MedTech.

6 July 2026

At TAM CERT, our activities today extend far beyond conformity assessment and certification. Through the Erasmus+ MedIT-HuB project, we play an active role in advancing digital health innovation, MedTech and digital health education, and international knowledge transfer, contributing to the development of future healthcare professionals and a stronger European MedTech ecosystem.

MedIT-HuB brings together universities, industry partners, and conformity assessment expertise to create practice-oriented educational programmes and foster long-term international collaboration.
