MedIT-HuB Successfully Completes Its First Reporting Period

An important milestone in the implementation of the MedIT-HuB Erasmus+ project.

31 May 2026

On 10 December 2025, the consortium partners of the MedIT-HuB project signed the Cooperation Agreement at Óbuda University, officially marking the launch of the “Building Capacities in Digital Health Innovation through Eastern-European Collaboration” initiative.

Consortium partners

  • TAM CERT Hungary Inspection and Certification Ltd.
  • Sapientia Hungarian University of Transylvania [EMTE] – Cluj-Napoca, Romania
  • EDU-PORT – Non-governmental Organisation, Subotica, Serbia

The project aims to support the advancement of healthcare and medical technology innovation, facilitate knowledge sharing, and strengthen cooperation among stakeholders within the European healthcare ecosystem. TAM CERT coordinates the project as the consortium leader.

Over the past months, we have worked closely with our partners through a series of professional meetings and study visits in Budapest, Subotica and Târgu Mureș.

These occasions provided valuable opportunities to jointly review the progress of the project, coordinate tasks for the upcoming work phases, advance the professional preparation of educational content development, and further strengthen cooperation among the consortium partners.

We are pleased to announce that the project's first reporting period was successfully completed on 31 May 2026.

The consortium fulfilled the professional, project management and implementation tasks planned for the reporting period, thereby establishing a solid foundation for the next phase of the project.

We would like to thank our partners for their dedicated work and cooperation. We are proud to contribute, through our expertise and international partnerships, to building a more innovative, safer and more sustainable future for healthcare.
