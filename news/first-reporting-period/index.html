MedIT-HuB: Successfully Completing the First Project Milestone

Three international partners, multiple study visits and professional meetings, and a successfully completed first reporting period in support of digital health innovation.

31 May 2026

Three international partners, multiple study visits and professional meetings, and a successfully completed first reporting period in support of digital health innovation.

On 10 December 2025, the consortium partners of the MedIT-HuB project signed the Cooperation Agreement at Óbuda University, officially marking the launch of the implementation of the “Building Capacities in Digital Health Innovation through Eastern-European Collaboration” initiative.

The consortium members are:

  • TAM CERT Hungary Inspection and Certification Ltd.
  • Sapientia Hungarian University of Transylvania [EMTE] (Universitatea Sapientia din Municipiul Cluj-Napoca | Cluj-Napoca, Romania)
  • EDU-PORT (Non-governmental Organisation | Subotica, Serbia)

The project aims to support the advancement of healthcare and medical technology innovation, promote knowledge sharing, and strengthen collaboration among stakeholders within the European healthcare ecosystem. The project is coordinated by TAM CERT as the lead consortium partner.

Over the past months, we have worked closely with our partners through a series of professional meetings and study visits in Budapest, Subotica, and Târgu Mureș. These occasions provided an opportunity to jointly review the project’s progress, coordinate the tasks of the upcoming work phases, advance the professional preparation related to curriculum development, and further strengthen our partnership cooperation.

We are pleased to share that the project’s first reporting period was successfully concluded on 31 May 2026. The consortium completed the professional and project management activities planned for the reporting period, thereby establishing a solid foundation for the implementation of the next phase of the project.

We would like to thank our partners for their commitment and cooperation. We are proud to contribute, through our expertise and international network, to building a more innovative, secure, and sustainable future for healthcare.
