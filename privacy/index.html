Terms & Privacy

Last updated: 3 March 2026

1. Privacy Policy

The MedIT-HuB project website is operated by TAM CERT Magyarország Vizsgáló és Tanúsító Kft. ("TAM CERT"), the coordinating organisation of MedIT-HuB. We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data through this website. We do not use web forms, account registration, analytics tracking, or cookies that collect personal information.

We only process personal data if you contact us directly by email. In that case, we may receive your email address, name/signature details, and any other personal information you include in your message.

Legal Basis for Processing

For direct email inquiries, we process personal data as necessary to respond to your request and manage communication (GDPR Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest), and where applicable on the basis of your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

Data Retention

Direct email communications are retained only as long as necessary to handle your inquiry and related follow-up, and may be retained longer if required by law.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, and port your personal data. You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, please contact us here.

2. No-Cookies Policy

This website does not use any cookies. We do not set essential cookies, preference cookies, analytics cookies, marketing cookies, or third-party cookies.

No cookie consent banner is used because no cookies are placed or read by this website.
